## 🎯 How to Use Each Section

### To Edit an Integration:

1. Click **"Edit"** button
2. You'll see a form with input fields
3. Enter your real credentials
4. Click **"Update Integration"**

### To Connect a New Service:

If you deleted the example integrations, you'll see connection forms where you can:

1. Enter your credentials
2. Check "Enable this integration"
3. Click "Connect [Service Name]"

### To Test Connection:

1. Click **"Test Connection"**
2. Wait for the result
3. See success or error message

### To Sync Orders (Shopify Only):

1. Click **"Sync Orders"**
2. Wait for sync to complete
3. Check your Orders page for imported orders

---

## 🔧 Example: Connecting Real Tawsilex

1. Click **"Edit"** on the Tawsilex card
2. Replace the example token with your real token from tawsilex.com
3. Click **"Update Integration"**
4. Click **"Test Connection"** to verify it works
5. Now you can create shipments from the Orders page!
