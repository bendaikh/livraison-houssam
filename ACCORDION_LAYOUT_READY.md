## 🖱️ How It Works

### 1. Click to Expand/Collapse

Click on any section header to expand or collapse it:

- **Expanded**: Shows green/orange/blue background + form/details
- **Collapsed**: Shows only the header with arrow pointing right (▶)
- **Arrow rotates** when you expand (points down ▼)

### 2. One Section at a Time

When you click a section:
- It expands
- The previously expanded section collapses
- Only one section is open at a time

### 3. Visual Feedback

- **Expanded section**: Colored background (green/orange/blue)
- **Collapsed section**: White background, hover shows gray
- **Status badge**: Shows "Active" or "Inactive"
- **Arrow icon**: Points right (▶) when collapsed, down (▼) when expanded
